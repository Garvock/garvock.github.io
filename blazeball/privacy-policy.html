Privacy Policy

BlazeBall · Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains what data BlazeBall ("the app") collects, why, and how you can control or remove it. BlazeBall is developed by Garvock Studios. If anything here is unclear, contact garvocksrivastava@gmail.com.

What we collect

Game data on your device

Your player profiles (names you type, coins, skills, best scores and settings) are saved on your device only. We don't run a server and never receive this data.

Google Play Games (optional)

If you sign in with Google Play Games, Google shares your Play Games player ID and display name with the app. The app uses them to link one profile to your account, to post your Solo scores to the public Play Games leaderboards, and to keep a cloud copy of that profile in Play Games Saved Games, so your progress can move to a new device. This data is stored by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; we don't keep a copy of it.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob: an occasional full-screen ad between games, and rewarded ads you can choose to watch for coins, a free skill or a retried throw. AdMob may collect your device's Advertising ID, IP address and information about ad interactions for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested, using Google's consent message; without consent, only non-personalised ads are shown. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Gameplay analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To see how the game is played and improve it, the app sends anonymous usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase: which screens are opened, when a game starts and ends (mode, number of balls, score, duration), skills used, purchases made with in-game coins, which ad offers were shown and watched, and whether Play Games sign-in worked. These events contain no names, profile names or contact details. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, analytics follows the consent choice you made in the app's consent message.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes or hits a serious error, a technical report (the screen and game mode you were in, the error itself, and device and Android version) is sent automatically so we can fix it. Crash reports don't include your game data or any personal information.

What we don't collect

The app never asks for your real name, email, phone number, location or contacts. There are no purchases — coins are earned by playing only — and no chat or messaging.

Your choices

  • Play without signing in to Google Play Games: everything stays on your device.
  • Change your ad consent at any time with Privacy options in the app's Settings (shown where consent applies).
  • Your consent choice in Privacy options also applies to gameplay analytics.
  • Reset or limit your Advertising ID in your device's Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).
  • Delete the cloud copy and leaderboard entries from your Google Play Games settings.
  • Uninstall the app to delete all data saved on your device.

Children

BlazeBall is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change too. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
