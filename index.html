TicTacRoll

Tic-Tac-Toe... but the dice decide where you play!

What is it?

Roll a die to pick your cell, then race to get three in a row. Play pass-and-play with a friend on one phone, against the computer, over local Wi-Fi, or online with a shareable game code — no sign-up needed for kids to jump in and play.

Got sent a game code? Open the link your friend shared — it'll open right up in TicTacRoll.
