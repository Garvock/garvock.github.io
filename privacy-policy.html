Privacy Policy

TicTacRoll · Last updated: 17 August 2026

This policy explains what data TicTacRoll ("the app") collects, why, and how you can control or remove it. TicTacRoll is developed by Garvock Studios. If anything here is unclear, contact garvocksrivastava@gmail.com.

What we collect

Gameplay analytics (Firebase Analytics)

Screen views, when a game starts/ends and how (mode, difficulty, outcome, duration), ad/reward/cosmetic events, and which kind of invite link was used to join a game. These events are anonymous — we do not collect names, contact details, or any way to identify you personally from this data.

Crash and error reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes or hits an internal error, a technical report (what screen you were on, the error itself, device/OS info) is sent automatically so we can fix bugs. These reports don't include your game data or any personal messages.

Online play data (Firebase Firestore)

If you play Online Multiplayer, Daily Challenge, or Solo Streak, we store your display name, win/loss record, and leaderboard entries, tied to your Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iOS) account — or to an anonymous "Guest" identity if you don't sign in. Live game state (board position, whose turn it is) is stored temporarily while a match is in progress.

Push notification token

If you turn on Notifications, your device registers a token with our server so we can send a "your turn!" push during an Online match. Turning Notifications off removes this token. (This feature may not be active in every build — see the app's Settings for its current status.)

Advertising

Local and vs-computer games show ads served by Google AdMob, and you can optionally watch a rewarded ad to unlock cosmetics or in-game perks. AdMob may use your device's Advertising ID for ad delivery and measurement. Because the game is suitable for all ages, ads are requested with child-directed treatment: they are non-personalized and rated for general audiences for every player. Online play is always ad-free.

What we don't collect

No real name, email, phone number, or physical address is ever requested by the app itself. No passwords are stored by us — sign-in is handled entirely by Google Play Games or Apple Game Center. No in-app chat or messaging exists, so there's nothing there to collect.

Your choices

  • Play entirely offline (Pass & Play, vs Computer, Local Wi-Fi) without any account or online data collection at all.
  • Turn Notifications off any time in Settings — this also removes your push token from our server.
  • Use the Erase Data button in Settings → App Setting to delete your local app data and, where applicable, your online stats and leaderboard entries.
  • Play as a Guest instead of signing in with Play Games/Game Center, if you'd rather not link an account.

Children

TicTacRoll is designed to be family-friendly and suitable for a general audience including children. We don't knowingly collect more than the anonymous gameplay/crash data described above, and ad personalization signals are off by default.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change too. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
